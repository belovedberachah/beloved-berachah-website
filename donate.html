Empower a Woman's Journey

Your generosity provides safe spaces, trauma-informed training, and vital community support for women rebuilding their lives.

Make a Lasting Impact

Beloved Berachah CIC relies on the kindness and financial partnership of people like you to continue our mission. Every contribution, no matter the size, is an investment in a woman's future.

When you choose to support us, you are directly funding educational talks, mentoring programmes, and community events that break the chains of isolation, shame, and trauma.

How Your Gift Helps

  • Education & Awareness Funding vital training resources and workshops for community groups and organisations.
  • Community Support Events Providing safe venues, refreshments, and materials for our awareness and recovery events.
  • Mentoring & Empowerment Allowing us to expand our capacity to encourage and mentor women stepping into their new beginnings.
