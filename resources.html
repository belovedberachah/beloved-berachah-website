Our Resources

We are developing a range of practical and educational resources to support individuals, churches, charities, organisations, and professionals working with women affected by trauma, addiction, domestic abuse, and mental health challenges.

Resources will include:

  • Educational guides and information sheets
  • Workshop materials and training resources
  • Reflection and devotional resources
  • Recommended support services and signposting information
  • Downloadable handouts and toolkits
  • Recovery, wellbeing, and self-care resources
  • Articles, blogs, and inspirational content

We will add new resources as they become available, so please check back regularly for updates.

Need Immediate Help?

Beloved Berachah provides ongoing support and workshops, but we are not a crisis service. If you are in immediate danger, please call 999. For 24/7 support, contact the organizations below.

Domestic Abuse Helpline
