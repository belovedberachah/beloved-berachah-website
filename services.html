Our Mission

We develop and deliver tailored programmes that empower individuals, boost confidence, and promote emotional wellbeing. Serving individuals affected by mental health challenges, trauma, and social isolation, our services are designed to foster healing, personal growth, and a sense of connection.

Informed by lived experience and professional knowledge, our sessions educate, encourage, and equip audiences with practical insights, greater understanding, and hope for healing, recovery, and restoration.

Who We Support

Our talks, workshops, and training are suitable for a wide range of organisations and audiences, including:

  • Churches and faith-based organisations
  • Charities and voluntary organisations
  • Women's groups and community organisations
  • Addiction recovery and support services
  • Mental health organisations and wellbeing services
  • Schools, colleges, universities, and training providers
  • Local authorities and community partnerships
  • NHS and other healthcare providers
  • Public sector organisations
  • Private sector organisations and businesses seeking to improve staff wellbeing, trauma awareness, and inclusive workplace practices

Our Core Services

We offer a range of supportive services designed to foster healing, personal growth, and connection.

Training Sessions

Our interactive sessions are designed to educate, challenge, and equip participants with practical knowledge and tools for personal growth.

Sample topics include:

  • Addictions
  • Healthy and Unhealthy Relationships
  • Identity
  • Negative Labelling
  • Understanding Domestic Abuse

Group Discussions

We offer safe, guided conversations that encourage reflection, connection, and peer support on real-life issues.

Sample discussion topics include:

  • Building Confidence
  • Overcoming Loneliness
  • Navigating Relationships

Creative Workshops

We believe creativity is a powerful tool for healing. It supports self-expression, reduces stress, and fosters joy. Our workshops feature hands-on activities such as creative writing, visual art, and various crafts that encourage emotional wellbeing and personal growth.

Drop-In Sessions

Our drop-in sessions provide a safe, welcoming space where individuals can relax, connect with others, and receive light-touch support in a friendly, low-pressure environment.

  • Tea & Coffee Mornings: A friendly space to unwind, chat, and connect with others.
  • Over 60s Gatherings: Social meetups designed to reduce isolation and promote wellbeing among older adults.
  • Women's Drop-Ins: Safe, supportive spaces for women to share, reflect, and access relevant resources.

Detailed Workshop Topics

Beloved Berachah CIC offers a comprehensive range of engaging talks, workshops, presentations, and training sessions. Each session can be tailored to the needs of your organisation, audience, or event.

Addiction

Understanding the root causes of addiction, the recovery journey, relapse prevention, and the hope of long-term healing and restoration.

Addiction Recovery

Exploring the practical, emotional, and psychological aspects of recovery, together with strategies for sustaining long-term wellbeing.

Healthy Relationships

Understanding the foundations of healthy relationships, including trust, respect, communication, healthy boundaries, and mutual support.

Unhealthy Relationships

Recognising unhealthy relationship patterns, manipulation, coercive control, and behaviours that can negatively affect emotional and psychological wellbeing.

Domestic Abuse Awareness

Recognising the different forms of domestic abuse, understanding its impact, responding appropriately, and knowing how to access support.

Dysfunctional Family

Exploring how dysfunctional family dynamics can influence identity, relationships, emotional wellbeing, and future life choices.

Emotional Wellbeing

Developing emotional resilience, self-awareness, healthy coping strategies, and practical approaches to improving mental and emotional wellbeing.

Faith and Restoration

Discovering hope, healing, identity, and purpose through faith, restoration, and personal transformation.

Freedom from Shame

Understanding the impact of shame, guilt, rejection, and past experiences, and discovering practical steps towards healing and freedom.

Discovering Your True Worth

Exploring identity, self-worth, belonging, purpose, and overcoming negative beliefs about yourself.

Lived Experience

Brenda Fraser shares her personal journey of trauma, addiction, recovery, healing, and restoration, alongside the lessons she has learned.

Negative Labels

Examining the impact of societal, cultural, family, and self-imposed labels, and how they can influence confidence, identity, and emotional wellbeing.

No More Bondage

Recognising emotional, spiritual, and behavioural strongholds, and exploring practical pathways towards lasting freedom, healing, and personal growth.

Overcoming Adversity at Work

Exploring how individuals can navigate workplace challenges, discrimination, and personal setbacks while developing resilience and confidence.

Peer Pressure

Understanding the influence of peer pressure, developing confidence, making healthy choices, and resisting negative influences.

Spiritual Abuse

Recognising spiritual abuse within faith settings, understanding its impact, and supporting healing, recovery, and restoration.

Trauma and Healing

Understanding the effects of trauma on the brain, body, emotions, relationships, and behaviour, while exploring trauma-informed approaches to healing and recovery.
