Services & Resources

Inspiring Hope, Healing, and Transformation

Beloved Berachah CIC delivers talks, workshops, and educational resources that raise awareness, reduce stigma, and empower individuals affected by trauma, addiction, domestic abuse, and mental health challenges.

Informed by lived experience and professional knowledge, our sessions educate, encourage, and equip audiences with practical insights, greater understanding, and hope for healing, recovery, and restoration.

Who We Support

Our talks, workshops, and training are suitable for a wide range of organisations and audiences, including:

  • Churches and faith-based organisations
  • Charities and voluntary organisations
  • Women's groups and community organisations
  • Addiction recovery and support services
  • Mental health organisations and wellbeing services
  • Schools, colleges, universities, and training providers
  • Local authorities and community partnerships
  • NHS and other healthcare providers
  • Public sector organisations
  • Private sector organisations and businesses seeking to improve staff wellbeing, trauma awareness, and inclusive workplace practices

Topics Available

Beloved Berachah CIC offers a range of engaging talks, workshops, presentations, and training sessions. Each session can be tailored to the needs of your organisation, audience, or event.

Addiction

Understanding the root causes of addiction, the recovery journey, relapse prevention, and the hope of long-term healing and restoration.

Addiction Recovery

Exploring the practical, emotional, and psychological aspects of recovery, together with strategies for sustaining long-term wellbeing.

Characteristics of a Healthy Relationship

Understanding the foundations of healthy relationships, including trust, respect, communication, healthy boundaries, and mutual support.

Characteristics of an Unhealthy Relationship

Recognising unhealthy relationship patterns, manipulation, coercive control, and behaviours that can negatively affect emotional and psychological wellbeing.

Domestic Abuse Awareness

Recognising the different forms of domestic abuse, understanding its impact, responding appropriately, and knowing how to access support.

Dysfunctional Family

Exploring how dysfunctional family dynamics can influence identity, relationships, emotional wellbeing, and future life choices.

Emotional Wellbeing

Developing emotional resilience, self-awareness, healthy coping strategies, and practical approaches to improving mental and emotional wellbeing.

Faith and Restoration

Discovering hope, healing, identity, and purpose through faith, restoration, and personal transformation.

Freedom from Shame

Understanding the impact of shame, guilt, rejection, and past experiences, and discovering practical steps towards healing and freedom.

Identity Crisis – Discovering Your True Worth

Exploring identity, self-worth, belonging, purpose, and overcoming negative beliefs about yourself.

Lived Experience

Brenda Fraser shares her personal journey of trauma, addiction, recovery, healing, and restoration, alongside the lessons she has learned through both lived experience and professional practice.

Negative Labels

Examining the impact of societal, cultural, family, and self-imposed labels, and how they can influence confidence, identity, opportunity, and emotional wellbeing.

No More Bondage

Recognising emotional, spiritual, and behavioural strongholds, and exploring practical pathways towards lasting freedom, healing, and personal growth.

Overcoming Adversity at Work

Exploring how individuals can navigate adversity, workplace challenges, discrimination, difficult experiences, and personal setbacks while developing resilience, confidence, and strategies to move forward.

Peer Pressure

Understanding the influence of peer pressure across different stages of life, developing confidence, making healthy choices, and resisting negative influences.

Spiritual Abuse

Recognising spiritual abuse within faith settings, understanding its impact, and supporting healing, recovery, and restoration.

Trauma and Healing

Understanding the effects of trauma on the brain, body, emotions, relationships, and behaviour, while exploring trauma-informed approaches to healing and recovery.
