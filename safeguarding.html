Our Commitment to Safety

At Beloved Berachah CIC, the safety, dignity, and wellbeing of the women we support are our highest priorities. We are committed to fostering an environment where vulnerable adults are protected from harm, abuse, and exploitation.

Core Principles

  • Zero Tolerance: We have a zero-tolerance approach to any form of abuse.
  • Empowerment: We support women to make their own informed decisions.
  • Accountability: All volunteers and staff are subject to DBS checks and mandatory safeguarding training.

Reporting a Concern

If you have any concerns regarding the safety of an individual involved with our services, please contact our Designated Safeguarding Lead immediately via our Contact page. In an emergency, always dial 999.
