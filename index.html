Our Vision Get Support

Beloved Berachah CIC is dedicated to supporting women affected by trauma, addiction, domestic abuse, and mental health challenges. Our mission is to bring hope, healing, and restoration to women who feel overlooked, unheard, isolated, misunderstood, or trapped by their circumstances.

Through lived experience, education, advocacy, and compassionate support, we empower women to rebuild their lives, rediscover their worth, and move forward with renewed hope and purpose.

The Story Behind Our Name

Our name, Beloved Berachah (Berachah is a Hebrew word meaning blessing or blessings), reflects our belief that every woman is precious, deeply valued, and created to live a life free from shame, fear, and emotional and spiritual bondage. We are committed to creating safe spaces where women can find encouragement, practical support, and opportunities to rebuild their lives, rediscover their identity, and embrace hope for the future.

"On the fourth day they assembled in the Valley of Berachah, where they blessed the Lord..."

What We Do

Beloved Berachah CIC provides a range of faith-informed and educational services designed to promote healing, recovery, resilience, and hope.

Educational Talks

Educational talks, keynote presentations, and interactive workshops covering trauma, addiction, recovery, mental health, emotional wellbeing, domestic abuse, identity, shame, and personal restoration.

Training Courses

In-person and online training courses for churches, charities, community organisations, schools, local authorities, and public and private sector organisations. Training is tailored to increase understanding, build confidence, reduce stigma, and equip participants to respond compassionately and effectively to individuals affected by trauma and related challenges.

Awareness Events

Awareness-raising events, conferences, seminars, and community outreach programmes that encourage informed conversations around trauma, addiction, domestic abuse, mental health, and recovery.

Mentoring & Empowerment

Encouragement, mentoring, and empowerment through lived experience, personal testimony, and practical insights that inspire hope and lasting change.

Community Initiatives

Community-based initiatives that foster connection, reduce isolation, and support women on their journey towards long-term healing, recovery, and restoration.

Signposting

Signposting to appropriate statutory, voluntary, faith-based, and specialist support services and resources.

Our Impact

We believe that recovery is possible, hope can be restored, and lives can be transformed. By sharing knowledge, raising awareness, and fostering supportive communities, we help women:

Break free from cycles of addiction and unhealthy behaviours.

Understand the impact of trauma and adverse life experiences.

Develop healthier coping strategies and build confidence, resilience, and self-worth.

Access appropriate support and growth opportunities.
