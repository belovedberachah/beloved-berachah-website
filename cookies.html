Legal Information

Cookie Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Beloved Berachah CIC uses cookies to improve your experience, analyse website traffic, and support our community outreach. This policy explains what cookies are, how we use them, and how you can manage your preferences.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you browse websites. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, provide a better user experience, and supply analytical information to the website owners.

How We Use Cookies

We classify our cookies into three categories in accordance with UK data protection regulations:

1. Essential Cookies

These cookies are strictly necessary to enable core functionality of the website, such as remembering your privacy preferences and securing the site. The website cannot function properly without these cookies, and they cannot be switched off in our systems.

2. Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. We use tools like Google Analytics to understand which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous.

3. Marketing & Third-Party Cookies

These cookies are set by third-party services that we embed on our website, such as YouTube videos or social media feeds. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant content on other sites. If you do not allow these cookies, you will not be able to view embedded third-party media on our site.

Managing Your Preferences

You have the right to withdraw your consent or change your cookie preferences at any time. You can do this easily by clicking the button below, or by selecting "Manage Cookies" in the footer of any page on this website.

Alternatively, most web browsers allow some control of most cookies through the browser settings. To find out more about cookies, including how to see what cookies have been set, visit www.aboutcookies.org.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about our use of cookies or this Cookie Policy, please contact us at beloved@berachah.org.uk.
