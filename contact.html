Brenda Fraser is available to deliver inspiring, trauma-informed talks, workshops, presentations, and training sessions tailored to the needs of your organisation or event.

Partnerships and Collaboration

We welcome opportunities to collaborate with a variety of organizations across different sectors.

• Churches and faith-based organisations
• Mental health and wellbeing organisations
• Charities and voluntary organisations
• Schools, colleges, universities, and training providers
• Community groups and community partnerships
• NHS and other healthcare providers
• Recovery and rehabilitation services
• Public sector organisations
• Local authorities and statutory services
• Private sector organisations committed to staff wellbeing, inclusion, and social responsibility

If you are interested in partnering with Beloved Berachah CIC or would like to explore a collaborative project, we would be delighted to hear from you. Please get in touch to arrange an initial discussion and explore how we can work together to support individuals, strengthen communities, and promote healing, recovery, and lasting positive change.
