How We Protect Your Data

Beloved Berachah CIC takes your privacy seriously. Due to the sensitive nature of our work, we adhere strictly to UK GDPR guidelines to ensure any personal or sensitive information you share with us is kept strictly confidential and highly secure.

Information We Collect

We only collect information that is absolutely necessary to provide you with support or fulfill our community objectives, including:

  • Contact details (when you voluntarily email us or subscribe to our newsletter).
  • Anonymised attendance data for grant reporting purposes.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your data, ask for it to be deleted, or withdraw consent at any time. For all privacy inquiries, please contact us.
